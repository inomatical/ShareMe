# 13 — Share images from BearShare

**What to build:** An Android user can share an image through BearShare and receives an independent delivery result once EagleShare completes persistence.

**Blocked by:** 05 — Establish BearShare target configuration; 08 — Share plain text from BearShare; 11 — Receive image uploads in EagleShare.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Android's standard share sheet offers BearShare for supported image content.
- [ ] BearShare streams the image without requiring the complete image in memory.
- [ ] EagleShare completion produces a success result, while delivery failure produces a failure result.
